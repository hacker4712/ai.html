Celebrating Mistakes

The mistake isn't the failure. Ignoring it is.

History's biggest discoveries didn't happen despite the mistake — they happened because someone studied it instead of throwing it away.

🔬 A mistake happens

A contaminated dish. A weak glue. An unexpected glow.

🧠 Someone studies it

Instead of discarding it, they ask "why did this happen?"

💡 It becomes a breakthrough

Antibiotics. Sticky notes. Medical imaging.

🤖 A hallucination happens

A fake citation. An invented policy. A wrong credit.

📝 We document it

Every case file is data about how the error formed.

✅ It becomes safer AI

Grounded answers, visible sources, honest uncertainty.
